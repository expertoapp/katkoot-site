A safe Arabic world where your child learns and plays

Katkoot («كتكوت») is an educational app for children aged 3–12 in Modern Standard Arabic: illustrated stories, Juz' Amma memorisation, tracing all 28 letters, colouring, 20 learning games, curated videos, private family competitions and a parents' area.

  • Works offline and collects no data
  • No ads, no in-app purchases, no music
  • Verified Uthmani Quran text
  • Gentle screen time that parents control
